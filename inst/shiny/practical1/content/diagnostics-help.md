Diagnostics flag model conditions that need careful interpretation.

- A **warning** asks you to treat the run as evidence with a caveat.
- A **caveat** is a short statement about what limits the conclusion.
- **No major diagnostic warnings** means the app did not detect a major numerical or biological warning for this run. It does not prove that the assumptions are correct or that risk is absent.
- **No observed extinctions** means none occurred in this finite set of simulated futures, not that extinction is impossible.
- A **single deterministic future** cannot describe probability or uncertainty; add stochasticity and multiple futures before making a risk statement.

Use diagnostics in your notes. They help you explain how strongly the result supports a conclusion.
