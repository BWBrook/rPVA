Read the risk plots first, then use the population plots to understand why the risk changed.

- **Extinction risk through time** is the cumulative proportion of simulated futures that have reached zero by each projection year.
- The **extinction-time histogram** shows when first extinction occurred, conditional on extinction occurring. A median marker appears only when at least half the futures are extinct.
- **Quasi-extinction risk** shows the chance that a future ever reaches each low-population threshold, from zero to 50% of the nominal habitat ceiling. It can reveal serious decline before complete extinction.
- A deterministic run with one future cannot estimate these probabilities. Quasi-extinction also needs a finite habitat ceiling.
- The **trajectory** plot shows individual population paths through time.
- The **summary interval** shows the central path and spread across futures.

Read plots beside the output tiles. A useful interpretation links the visual pattern to PE, Nmed, and uncertainty.
