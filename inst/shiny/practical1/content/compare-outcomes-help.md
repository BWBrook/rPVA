# Reading the outcome matrix

Read the rows together rather than choosing a single favourite output.

- **Extinction probability** is the proportion of simulated futures that reached zero within the projection period.
- **Median final abundance** describes the middle final population size.
- The **95% interval** shows the spread among simulated futures.
- Baseline-relative changes show the direction and size of the contrast.

A lower median, wider interval, or higher extinction probability can each matter. Interpret a zero observed extinction probability in light of the time horizon, number of futures, and model assumptions.
