# Choosing evidence

Use three different roles: **baseline, risk mechanism, and intervention or vortex**.

- The first baseline run stays locked as the common reference.
- The risk mechanism should isolate a pressure that could worsen viability.
- The intervention or vortex run should show either a possible response or several pressures acting together.

Choose runs that answer a focused question. A later selector change updates the final comparison, but it does not rewrite observations already saved in your notebook.
