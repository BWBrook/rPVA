## How the route works

The five **Core** missions build a working population model in sequence. Each successful Core run carries its settings forward. The three **Explore Further** missions start from your latest successful Core settings and add one further threat mechanism.

Explore changes do not rewrite your Core working model. Their mechanism settings carry forward into later Explore missions, culminating in the combined Vortex comparison. To change a shared setting such as projection years, habitat ceiling, simulation futures, environmental variation, mortality, or fecundity, return to the relevant Core mission and run it again.

## Core missions

### 1. Baseline compass

Build the deterministic reference. With fixed vital rates, the same assumptions produce the same trajectory. Change projection years, mortality, or fecundity only when you want a different reference projection.

### 2. Island ceiling

Add a habitat ceiling and variation in that ceiling. The ceiling limits breeding and territory-holding stages, so juveniles and floaters can make total population size exceed the displayed ceiling.

### 3. Individual chance

Add demographic stochasticity: births and deaths differ among individuals by chance. Simulation futures control how many possible trajectories are sampled.

### 4. Environmental variability

Add shared bonanza and bust years that affect individuals together. Greater environmental variation usually widens the range of possible futures and may increase extinction risk.

### 5. Rescue attempt

Test a habitat improvement against the accumulated risk model. Ask whether the intervention changes extinction risk, final abundance, and uncertainty, rather than looking only for population growth.

## Explore Further

### Small-population pressure

Add starting inbreeding to the Core model. This explores whether genetic pressure compounds the demographic and environmental risks already present.

### Rare bad years

Add uncommon catastrophes with specified frequency and survival severity. This tests whether rare events dominate risk even when ordinary years appear manageable.

### Vortex comparison

Combine several small-population threats in one scenario. Use it to examine reinforcement among demographic chance, environmental variability, genetics, and catastrophes.
