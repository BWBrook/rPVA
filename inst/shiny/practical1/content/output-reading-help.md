Use the output tiles and Settings record as a quick evidence checklist.

- **scenario** names the mechanism represented by the current run.
- **years** is the projection horizon used for this run.
- **PE** is extinction risk: the proportion of simulation futures that reached zero.
- **Nmed** is the median final abundance across the futures.
- **uncertainty interval** shows the spread of likely final abundances.
- A **fixed deterministic projection** has only one future, so it cannot estimate a probability or uncertainty distribution.
- **PE = 0** means zero observed extinctions in the simulation futures you ran. Treat it as evidence from this run and keep the assumptions in view.
- The **Settings record** compares the selected mission with pinned baseline run 1. It shows what was added, removed, or changed before you interpret the outcome.

A strong note names the input you changed, the output that moved, and the ecological reason the change matters.
