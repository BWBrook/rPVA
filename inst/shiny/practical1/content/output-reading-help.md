Use the output tiles as a quick evidence checklist.

- **PE** is extinction risk: the proportion of simulation futures that reached zero.
- **Nmed** is the median final abundance across the futures.
- **uncertainty interval** shows the spread of likely final abundances.
- **PE = 0** means zero observed extinctions in the simulation futures you ran. Treat it as evidence from this run and keep the assumptions in view.

A strong note names the input you changed, the output that moved, and the ecological reason the change matters.
