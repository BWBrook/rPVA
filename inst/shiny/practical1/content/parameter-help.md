Each mission changes a small part of the model. The controls are the inputs you are testing.

- **habitat ceiling** limits the combined abundance of the breeding and territory-holding stages (subadults and adults). Juveniles or floaters are not counted, so the total population can exceed the ceiling.
- **yearly ceiling variation** changes that habitat ceiling among years.
- **individual chance** switches on random births and deaths, so otherwise identical futures can diverge.
- **environmental variation** makes survival and breeding move together through bonanza and bust years.
- **starting inbreeding** begins the run with extra genetic pressure already present.
- **catastrophe chance** sets how often a rare damaging event occurs, separately from ordinary environmental variation.
- **simulation futures** sets how many possible futures the app runs for a stochastic stage.
- **projection years**, **mortality change**, and **fecundity change** let you test the time horizon or bounded vital-rate sensitivities.

Change one control at a time when you can. Then compare the new run with baseline run 1 and name the biological mechanism you tested.
