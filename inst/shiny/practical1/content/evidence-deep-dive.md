The evidence pack is your record of what you tested and how you interpreted it. Build it after you have useful comparisons; you do not need to save something after every click.

- A **comparison CSV** records the runs side by side, including the baseline, the changed stage, extinction risk, abundance, and uncertainty. You can open this in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or as a plain text file.
- An **evidence notebook** is a Markdown file containing your final synthesis, every saved notebook observation and its metric snapshot, the final three-run comparison, and settings provenance.
- **Plot PNGs** are optional visual evidence. Extinction, quasi-extinction, trajectory, and summary plots can be saved from the current run.
- A **notebook observation** explains what one selected comparison shows: what changed, which output supports it, and what uncertainty or caveat matters.
- A **final synthesis** draws the pattern across your strongest observations and states what they jointly support.

The browser session is temporary. Before closing the tab, use Transfer to download both files and confirm that each is marked **Current**. The evidence you gather is there to support your explanation, helps you consider trade-offs, and gives you something concrete to return to later.
