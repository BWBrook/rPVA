The evidence pack is your record of what you tested and how you interpreted it. Save it after you have a useful comparison (no need to do it after every click!).

- A **comparison CSV** records the runs side by side, including the baseline, the changed stage, extinction risk, abundance, and uncertainty. You can open this in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or as a plain text file.
- An **evidence-notes file** records settings and prompts from the same browser session, including the Settings record behind each selected run. It uses Markdown, a plain-text format that opens in any text editor.
- **Plot PNGs** are optional visual evidence. Extinction, quasi-extinction, trajectory, and summary plots can be saved from the current run.
- A **one-sentence interpretation** is your job: say what changed, which output supports it, and what uncertainty or caveat matters. Keeping notes of your thought process is important.

The browser session is temporary, so request the comparison CSV and evidence notes before you close the tab. The evidence you gather is there to support your explanation, helps you consider trade-offs, and gives you something concrete to return to later.
