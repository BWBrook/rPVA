Your evidence pack is a small record of what you tested and how you interpreted it. Save it after you have a useful comparison, not after every click.

- A **comparison CSV** records the runs side by side, including the baseline, the changed stage, extinction risk, abundance, and uncertainty.
- An **evidence-notes Markdown** file records settings and prompts from the same browser session.
- A **one-sentence interpretation** is your job: say what changed, which output supports it, and what uncertainty or caveat matters.

The evidence is not the answer by itself. It supports your explanation, helps the class discuss tradeoffs, and gives you something concrete to return to later.
