## Question

What might have happened if managers had taken no new action in 1980?

Only about **4-6 viable breeding pairs** remained, largely restricted to upland
habitat where pigs were absent. The model assumes that this small refuge could
support about **10 breeding territories**, plus younger birds that had not yet
secured a territory.

## How to read it

This is a counterfactual: it models a plausible history that did not actually
occur. Use it as the common no-action comparison for the two management cases.
High extinction risk would show why delay was dangerous, not prove the exact
fate the real population would have experienced.
