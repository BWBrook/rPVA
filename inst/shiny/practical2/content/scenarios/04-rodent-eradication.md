## Question

Can the model reproduce the rapid recovery observed after rodent eradication?

The scenario begins with the reported **208 birds in 2019** and projects five
years to 2024. Rodent removal is represented by more available habitat, higher
early-life survival, and about a 50% increase in recruitment. These changes
stand in for reduced egg and chick predation and improved food availability.

## How to read it

The survey reported **more than 1,600 birds by 2024**. Compare that milestone
with the model trajectory and uncertainty interval. This is a short historical
check of direction and scale, not an estimate of the separate effect of every
ecological process. Do not rank it against the 40-year 1980 scenarios.
