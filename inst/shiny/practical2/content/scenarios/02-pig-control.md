## Question

How much difference might pig control have made without captive breeding?

Pigs damaged vegetation, preyed on wildlife, and restricted Woodhens to the
uplands. This scenario represents pig control by increasing the habitat ceiling
from about **10 to 70 breeding territories**, with environmental variation
around that ceiling. The ceiling applies to territory-holding breeding adults,
not to every bird, so total abundance can be greater than 70 pairs.

## How to read it

Compare this scenario directly with **No intervention**. The model does not
simulate pig numbers or habitat recovery in detail; the larger habitat ceiling
stands in for the ecological benefit of successful pig control.
