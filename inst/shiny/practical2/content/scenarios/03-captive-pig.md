## Question

What changes when captive breeding and release are added to pig control?

This scenario keeps the larger habitat ceiling used for pig control. It also
represents the removal of some last wild birds into captivity in 1981-82 and
modelled releases of captive-bred birds across 1982-84. The first-decade plot
marks these periods so you can connect the intervention timing to the simulated
population response.

## How to read it

Compare all three 1980 scenarios. This is a **combined intervention**, so its
outcome cannot by itself separate the contribution of pig control from captive
breeding. It asks whether the package of actions could plausibly shift the
population away from immediate extinction risk.
