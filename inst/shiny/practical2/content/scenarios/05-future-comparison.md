## Question

What does the model project from 2025 to 2100 if no new pressure or management
intervention is added?

This scenario begins from the recovered 2024 population and retains the
ecological conditions used in the rodent-recovery model. It provides the common
comparison for every sensitivity, threat, and intervention family in Phase 2.

## How to read it

This is a **no-change comparison**, not a forecast that the island will remain
unchanged for 75 years. Examine both extinction and the chance of falling below
**50 breeding pairs**. The 50-pair threshold is an early-warning level: managers
would need to respond well before the last Woodhen disappeared.
