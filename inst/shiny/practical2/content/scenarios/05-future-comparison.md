## Question

What does the model project from 2025 to 2100 if no new pressure or management
intervention is added?

This scenario begins from a model population close to the observed 2024 scale
and retains the ecological conditions used in the rodent-recovery model. Its
starting stage distribution is not an exact reconstruction of the 2024 survey.
It provides the common comparison for every sensitivity, threat, and
intervention family in Phase 2.

Habitat availability is represented as an approximate ceiling on
territory-holding females, used here as a proxy for breeding pairs. The total
abundance plot also includes young non-breeding birds, so it can temporarily
rise well above twice that pair ceiling.

## How to read it

This is a **no-change comparison**, not a forecast that the island will remain
unchanged for 75 years. Examine both extinction and the chance of falling below
**50 breeding pairs**. The 50-pair threshold is a shared working warning level
for this practical, not a universal biological tipping point. It lets you
compare scenarios at a stage when managers would need to respond well before
the last Woodhen disappeared.
