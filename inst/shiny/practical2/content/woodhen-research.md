## Research the Woodhen

The supplied case material is the common starting point. You are encouraged, but not required, to strengthen your investigation with relevant scientific and management sources.

Useful directions include Woodhen ecology and demography, habitat and
monitoring, historical and current threats, management history, plausible
future pressures, and evidence relevant to model assumptions or omitted
processes.

Use wider research to improve ecological reasoning rather than to add an
unrelated natural-history summary. Evaluate the relevance and quality of any
sources you use and cite them appropriately. There is no arbitrary source
quota.
