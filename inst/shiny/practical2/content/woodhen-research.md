## Research the Woodhen

The supplied case material is the common starting point. Wider research is
optional and can strengthen your investigation. Use good scientific citation
practice by selecting relevant, credible scientific and management sources.
Attach citations to the claims they support.

Useful directions include Woodhen ecology and demography, habitat and
monitoring, historical and current threats, management history, plausible
future pressures, and evidence relevant to model assumptions or omitted
processes. Research population viability analysis as a quantitative tool for
extinction-risk assessment and conservation decision-making.

Select enough high-quality sources to support the claims and ecological
reasoning in your report. Within the approximately 2,000-word report, use this
wider context selectively. The word limit rewards concise, well-written,
selective synthesis. Additional research should strengthen your argument and
stay focused on the conservation decision.
