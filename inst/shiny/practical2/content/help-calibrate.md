## What this calibration can support

The reference Woodhen scenarios are historically grounded teaching cases. A
model that reproduces important historical patterns earns some confidence as a
tool for exploratory future inference, but historical agreement **supports, but does not prove**, that fitness. Several parameter combinations can produce
similar outcomes, and a reference scenario is not a causal estimate of one
management action.

Compare the three 1980 cases directly because they share the decision context,
starting state, 40-year horizon, seed 123, and 1,000 simulated futures. Focus on
extinction probability, abundance trajectories, final abundance, and the
simulation interval. Their starting populations are already below 50 breeding
pairs, so a year-zero 50-pair crossing is not useful comparative evidence.

## The short recovery check

The 2019-2024 rodent-eradication case is a five-year historical check. The
observed historical milestone was **over 1,600 birds by 2024**. The exact
**1,654 birds** and **about 330 breeding pairs** shown in the app describe the
supplied 2024 model state; they are not presented as an independently precise
empirical census.

This short run **must not be ranked** against the three 40-year cases. Use it to
ask whether the simulated recovery direction and scale are compatible with the
observed history, while retaining uncertainty about attribution and model
structure.

## The locked future baseline

The 2025-2100 no-change case uses the supplied 2024 starting vector, 75 years,
1,000 simulated futures, seed 123, and unchanged reference settings. Completing
this exact run establishes the locked future baseline required for Act 2. For
this future case, both extinction risk and the shared risk of falling below 50
territory-holding breeding pairs are relevant.

empty never blocks a run or completion.
