## Ways to deepen your investigation

Build analytical depth through justified choices, careful interpretation,
selectivity, and consequential work. Add complexity only when it strengthens
the conservation argument.

Optional routes include examining a sensitivity factor more systematically;
comparing alternative intervention strengths or timings; testing robustness to
stochastic variation or an analytical threshold; examining interacting threats
or actions; identifying monitoring and information priorities; or connecting
model assumptions more strongly to published ecological evidence.

Choose the routes that add clear value to your reasoning. Assessment
requirements and marks remain those stated in the task criteria and MyLO.
