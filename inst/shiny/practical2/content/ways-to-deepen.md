## Ways to deepen your investigation

Depth comes from justification, interpretation, selectivity, and consequence,
not from complexity for its own sake.

You could examine a sensitivity factor more systematically; compare alternative
intervention strengths or timings; test robustness to stochastic variation or
an analytical threshold; examine interacting threats or actions; identify
monitoring and information priorities; or connect model assumptions more
strongly to published ecological evidence.

These are possibilities, not additional requirements or promises about marks.
Choose only work that strengthens the conservation argument.
