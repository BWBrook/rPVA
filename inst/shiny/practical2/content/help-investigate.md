## Common future anchor

Both workspaces require the current common future comparison from Calibrate.
Each completed family then reruns an identical baseline within that family's own
comparison ancestry. This preserves the supplied 2024 starting population, 75
projection years, 1,000 simulated futures, stage structure, and all
non-target settings.

## Sensitivity

Choose one ecological factor and compare its locked baseline with one higher
and one lower setting. The displayed bounds are deliberately wide enough to
reveal model responses and support exploration; they are guardrails, not a
claim that every permitted value is realistic. The two starting alternatives
are bounded teaching assumptions, not empirical estimates or forecasts. A
custom value must stay within the displayed catalogue bounds. Interpret
vector-backed survival and fecundity settings as multipliers; their locked
baseline is 1.

## Future advice

The comparison ancestry is locked baseline, then threat, then intervention.
An intervention difference is therefore relative to its threatened parent.
Ordinary threats are immediate persistent steps from year 1. Sea-level rise is
the explicit exception: it is represented as a gradual linear habitat-capacity
pathway across years 1-75, not as an instantaneous shock or a sea-level
parameter.

## Risk and timing

The common warning event is **strictly fewer than 100 territory-holding
breeding pairs**. It sums territory-holding stages 2 onward, excludes stage-1
floaters, and records first passage in elapsed years 0-75. This working
threshold is approximately the pre-rodent-eradication population scale, but it
is not a direct conversion of historical total-bird counts, a universal minimum
viable population, or a proven biological tipping point.

Investigate plots focus on this warning risk because complete extinction is
usually rare under the bounded scenarios. Extinction probability remains in
the results tables. The conditional median warning time describes only futures
that cross. The overall median is shown only when at least half of all futures
cross; otherwise it is not reached.

## Notes and deeper work

Empty prompts never block a run. Non-empty pre-run prompts are recorded only
after a whole family succeeds, and an exact rerun does not duplicate the same
observation. Interpretation and caveat fields can be saved after results.

Advanced multi-factor scenarios and timing controls are not yet implemented.
They are optional later headroom, not part of this core route.
