# Practical 2 Student Launch Page

## Woodhen Management Lab

Today you will use PVA evidence to compare management options for the Lord Howe
Island woodhen. Your goal is to turn model output into conservation advice that
a manager could understand.

## What to open

- The approved Practical 2 Woodhen Management Lab app link supplied in class.
- This launch page.
- A local evidence folder for your CSV and Markdown exports.
- Your Practical 1 notes if you need a reminder of PE, abundance, uncertainty,
  and one-factor comparisons.

## What you will do

1. Run the core Woodhen management scenarios.
2. Compare extinction risk, final abundance, and uncertainty.
3. Run a bounded sensitivity set where one factor changes at a time.
4. Save the comparison CSV and evidence-notes Markdown.
5. Draft one evidence-backed recommendation sentence.

## Evidence to save

Save these files from the app:

- `woodhen-management-comparison-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`
- `woodhen-management-evidence-notes-YYYY-MM-DD.md`

Also keep one short note that states:

- which scenario looked strongest,
- which sensitivity setting changed your confidence,
- which caveat matters most for your recommendation.

## Report bridge

Use this pattern when drafting your Assessment Task 1 report:

> The strongest support for _[management option]_ is _[quantitative evidence]_;
> this matters because _[biological interpretation]_, with _[caveat]_ limiting
> confidence.

Your final report should sound like conservation advice. Use model results as
evidence, then explain what they mean biologically.
